Repair your doorwall instead of replacing it

In Michigan, a doorwall (also written door wall) is a sliding glass patio door. A heavy panel that sticks or grinds does not necessarily need replacement. Worn rollers, debris, a damaged rail or misalignment can make a door difficult to move. We assess the panel and hardware to determine whether repair is practical.

Our doorwall repair services

Doorwall roller replacement

Replace compatible worn wheels and adjust the panel so it rolls correctly. Grinding, dragging and uneven movement are common signs of roller problems.

Doorwall track repair

Assess damaged rails, buildup and uneven tracks. Depending on the condition, cleaning, adjustment or a suitable track repair may restore smooth operation.

Doorwall lock repair

Inspect latches, alignment and compatible locking hardware when the patio door will not close or lock properly.

Doorwall handle repair

Address loose, damaged or malfunctioning compatible handles and related hardware.

Common doorwall problems in Michigan

Cold weather, moisture, dirt and repeated use can make an existing hardware problem more noticeable. If your doorwall is frozen shut, avoid forcing the panel. If it is off track, unstable or has damaged glass, stop using it until it can be assessed.

Frequently asked questions

Is a doorwall the same as a sliding glass door?

Yes. Doorwall and door wall are regional terms commonly used in Michigan for a sliding glass patio door.

Can a doorwall that is hard to open be repaired?

Often, yes. The cause may be worn rollers, track damage or misalignment. Repair depends on the condition of the frame, panel and availability of compatible parts.

How much does doorwall repair cost?
