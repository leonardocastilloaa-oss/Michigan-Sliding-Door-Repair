What Is a Window Balance?

A window balance counteracts the weight of the sash so it stays where you leave it. When a spring or balance shoe fails, the window may fall, lean, bind or refuse to open properly.

Types of Balances

We inspect channel (block-and-tackle), spiral and coil/constant-force balance systems. Correct replacement depends on the dimensions, fitting style and weight rating; a similar-looking part may not be compatible.

Our Repair Process

We inspect the sash and guides, identify the balance assembly and related pivot shoes or tilt parts, discuss compatible replacement options, then test operation after installation. Some hardware may require ordering.

When Repair May Not Be Enough

A severely damaged sash, frame or unavailable proprietary component can make a hardware-only repair impractical. We do not offer glass replacement or complete window installation.
