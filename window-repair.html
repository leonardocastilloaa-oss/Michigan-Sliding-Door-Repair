Why This Happens

What Causes Window Repair Problems

Understanding why your door or window fails helps you recognize problems early — and know when it's time to call a professional before minor issues become expensive repairs.

Balance failure — the #1 window problem in Metro Detroit

Single and double-hung windows use internal spring or block-and-tackle balances to hold the sash in any open position. Metro Detroit's heat accelerates the degradation of balance springs — the constant expansion and contraction cycles the springs go through in Metro Detroit's climate wear them out 30-40% faster than in cooler regions. A failed balance is why your window falls closed on its own or won't stay open.

Salt air corrosion on hardware

Window locks, handles, operators and hinge hardware are particularly vulnerable to Metro Detroit's road salt and damp winter air. Metal components corrode, seize and fail — especially in homes within a few miles of the coast. Aluminum frames can also develop oxidation that causes the window to bind in the frame.

Frame warping from heat and humidity

Metro Detroit's extreme heat causes window frames to expand and contract daily. Over time this repeated movement causes frames to warp slightly — enough to create binding points where the window sticks, won't close fully or leaves gaps that allow water infiltration during rain.

Impact window hardware wear

Michigan's building code requires impact-rated windows in most construction — and impact window hardware takes more stress than standard windows. The heavier glass puts more load on balances, hinges and locks, wearing them out faster. Impact window hardware is also more expensive and specialized, making professional repair important.

Warning Signs

Signs You Need Repair Now

Don't wait until the door completely fails. These are the warning signs that repair is needed:

  • ✓Window falls down or won't stay open in any position
  • ✓Window won't open or is very difficult to operate
  • ✓Lock doesn't fully engage or the window can be pushed open when locked
  • ✓Window rattles in its frame during wind or rain
  • ✓Water is coming in through the frame or around the glass during rain
  • ✓The window is visibly crooked or gaps appear around the frame
Prevention

How to Prevent Problems

Simple maintenance habits extend the life of your doors and windows significantly in Metro Detroit's demanding climate:

1
Clean window tracks and channels annually

Dirt and debris in window channels causes binding that puts extra stress on balances and hardware. A clean channel means less wear.

2
Lubricate moving parts

Apply silicone lubricant to window channels and hardware annually. This reduces friction, prevents corrosion and extends hardware life.

3
Inspect seals and weatherstripping

Check the perimeter seal annually — especially after hurricane season. Damaged seals allow water infiltration that can damage the frame and wall below.

4
Don't force a sticking window

Forcing a stuck window breaks balances, strips hardware and can crack the frame. If it doesn't open smoothly, have it inspected.
