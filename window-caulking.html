When Does a Window Need New Caulking?

Gaps, cracks, peeling sealant, brittle joints or separated beads around a window frame may let rain and outside air enter. We inspect accessible joints to determine whether removal and replacement of damaged sealant is appropriate.

What Our Window Caulking Service Includes

For suitable residential jobs, we inspect accessible window-to-trim or window-to-siding joints, remove failed caulk where needed, prepare the surface, apply compatible exterior-grade sealant and finish the joint. Scope and sealant selection depend on the materials, joint movement, and site conditions.

Water, Drafts and Moisture

A leaking window is not always a caulking problem. Water can arrive from flashing, siding, roofing, concealed wall conditions or the window unit itself. We assess visible and accessible joints and explain when a different specialist is needed.

What We Do Not Promise

We do not claim that caulking can correct every leak, perform invasive building-envelope reconstruction, replace glass or install full windows. We do not seal designed drainage or weep openings.
