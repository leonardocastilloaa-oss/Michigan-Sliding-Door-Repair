Where Do Window Drafts Come From?

Air can enter at failed perimeter sealant joints, gaps between trim and frames, worn operating seals or other wall connections. An assessment helps identify whether caulking is appropriate.

Our Caulking and Gap-Sealing Work

For accessible, appropriate joints, we remove failed sealant, prepare the surface and re-caulk with a product compatible with the frame and adjoining material. We do not cover weep holes or drainage channels.

What Else Might Be Needed?

Some drafts come from deteriorated weatherstripping, frame defects or hidden installation problems. Re-caulking exterior joints does not solve every type of draft or guarantee a measurable energy saving.
