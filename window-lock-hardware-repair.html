Window Won't Latch?

Misalignment, a worn sash lock, missing keeper or damaged tilt hardware may prevent a window from fastening properly. The cause should be identified before parts are replaced.

Parts and Repairs

We inspect compatible sash locks, keepers, tilt latches and selected operating hardware on residential windows. Hardware selection depends on the window design and dimensions.

Security and Operation

A window that cannot latch should be addressed promptly. If the sash is out of square or binding, a lock replacement alone may not solve it.

What We Don't Service

Our window offering is hardware repair, not cracked-glass replacement, failed insulated glass seals or whole-window installation.
