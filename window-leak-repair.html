Why Is Water Coming In Around My Window?

Water stains, damp drywall and wet trim can have several causes: failed exterior sealant, cracked joints, defective flashing, roof or siding leaks, or drainage problems. Finding the path of the water matters more than applying extra caulk.

What We Can Assess and Repair

We inspect visible and accessible perimeter joints and evaluate whether failed exterior caulking or sealant can reasonably explain water entry. When appropriate, we remove and replace compromised caulking and re-seal accessible window-to-trim or window-to-siding joints.

When Caulking Is Not the Answer

If water is entering behind the siding, through flashing, roof areas or concealed structural assemblies, surface caulking alone is unlikely to resolve it. We can explain the visible findings and recommend evaluation by the appropriate building-envelope specialist.

Signs Worth Investigating

Peeling caulk at an exterior frame joint, localized moisture following rain, staining around trim and drafts near the frame can justify an assessment. If water is active near electrical outlets, keep clear and seek urgent qualified help.
