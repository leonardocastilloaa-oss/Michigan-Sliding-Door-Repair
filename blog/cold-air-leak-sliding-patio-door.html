Why Cold Air Leaks Around Sliding Patio Doors in Michigan

Cold drafts near a patio door do not always mean the glass itself is failing. Gaps around the moving panel can admit outside air. This guide explains what to observe, what to avoid, and when a professional evaluation is appropriate.

Start with the symptoms

On a windy day, feel for air around the meeting stile and lower edge without placing hands in a closing door. Check weatherstripping for flattened or missing sections.

What a repair professional will consider

Roller height and panel alignment influence whether the seals meet correctly. Adjusting a door that sits crooked may improve sealing, but worn weatherstripping can need replacement.

Safety and common mistakes

Avoid sealing a functioning drainage opening with caulk. Patio door frames often depend on intentional water escape paths.

Practical next step: Note exactly where the draft is felt so an inspection can focus on the seal or hardware.

What this means for Michigan homeowners

Across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties, seasonal moisture, freezing conditions, and everyday usage can affect door performance in different ways. Not every symptom is caused by winter weather, and the safest solution depends on the age, frame condition, and hardware installed. Keep notes about when the problem occurs and how it changes after simple cleaning. These details can make a service assessment more effective.

When to request service

If the sliding panel remains difficult to operate, its latch fails, the rail is visibly damaged, or a heavy glass panel appears misaligned, avoid forcing it. Request an assessment of the rollers, track, and related hardware.
