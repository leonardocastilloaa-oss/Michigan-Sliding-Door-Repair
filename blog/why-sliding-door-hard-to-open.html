Why Is My Sliding Door So Hard to Open?

A difficult patio door may have worn rollers, a bent track, debris, or winter ice.

What to check first

A door that catches in one position may be crossing a dented rail. Resistance throughout the whole opening can indicate worn bearings or general misalignment. Begin with a visual check and gentle track cleaning.

When a repair is worth considering

A professional should examine the rolling assemblies and track before recommending parts. Replacing a handle will not correct a heavy panel that is straining the latch.

Safety comes first

Avoid forcing, levering, or removing heavy glass panels without proper support.

Michigan homeowners: plan the next step

Seasonal ice and moisture can temporarily change how a patio door moves, but regular grinding and alignment problems call for an examination of its mechanical components. Document symptoms, and ask what parts will be repaired, adjusted, or replaced. Metro Detroit homes vary widely in door size and hardware. A qualified assessment should account for panel weight, frame integrity, and parts availability.
